About the Lab

The Human-Technology Interaction (HTI) Lab investigates how humans interact with emerging technologies and how intelligent systems can better understand and respond to human cognitive, physiological, and behavioral states.

Our research combines human-computer interaction, artificial intelligence, wearable sensing, affective computing, and multimodal interaction.

Research Areas

Human-AI Interaction

Studying how people interact with intelligent systems and AI assistants.

Wearable Biosensing

Using physiological signals to understand human states and behavior.

Affective Computing

Understanding human emotions and affective states through multimodal sensing.

Cognitive Load

Investigating human cognitive states during interaction with technology.
