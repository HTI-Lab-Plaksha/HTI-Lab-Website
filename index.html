ABOUT THE LAB

Understanding the relationship between humans and technology.

The Human-Technology Interaction (HTI) Lab at Plaksha University studies how people and technology interact, with a focus on enhancing human cognitive well-being through a multi-modal sensory approach.

We combine bio-sensors, computer vision, and electro-mechanical sensing to detect and model changes in physiological and behavioral responses. These include neural activity, facial expressions, heart-rate variability, pupillometry, and galvanic skin response, alongside human ergonomics and cognitive state.

Our work is inherently interdisciplinary, drawing on human factors, data science, and public policy to understand the complex interplay between technology and human cognition.

This allows us to move from raw physiological signals to actionable insights about how people actually experience the systems they use.

Our research brings together artificial intelligence, sensing, human factors, and computational approaches to understand human-technology interaction.

Developing intelligent systems and computational approaches for understanding and supporting human interaction with technology.

Applying machine learning methods to physiological, behavioral, and multimodal data.

Using visual sensing and computer vision to understand human behavior and interaction.

Exploring computational approaches to understand neural activity and its relationship with cognition and behavior.

MULTI-MODAL SENSING

From physiological signals to human insight.

Our research integrates multiple sensing modalities to capture rich information about human physiological, behavioral, and cognitive states.

Neural Activity

Understanding neural responses during interaction.

Facial Expressions

Analyzing facial behavior and affective responses.

Heart-Rate Variability

Measuring physiological responses and autonomic activity.

Pupillometry

Investigating cognitive and physiological responses.

Galvanic Skin Response

Measuring physiological arousal and response.

Human Ergonomics

Understanding physical interaction and human performance.
