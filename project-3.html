ONGOING PROJECT

EKAGRA AI

Pedagogical Effectiveness Across a Learner’s SOLO Transition State. Investigating how teaching approaches influence the development of a learner’s understanding across successive SOLO transitions.

PROJECT

Problem Statement

AI tutors are increasingly designed to adapt not only what they teach but also how they teach. However, selecting an appropriate teaching approach requires understanding which learner characteristics should guide that decision. Most existing evidence describes learners based on how much they already know. A study of one million students on an online tutoring system found that personalizing based on learner features may add little over well-optimized one-size-fits-all policies. What remains largely unexplored is the structure of a learner's understanding: how well their ideas are connected and integrated. The SOLO taxonomy describes this progression through five levels:

RESEARCH QUESTION

Our Research Question

Does the best teaching approach change depending on which SOLO transition a learner is attempting, and does it change as the same learner moves through successive transitions?

METHODOLOGY

What We Are Doing

We are designing a study that compares three teaching approaches at each SOLO transition. The study follows the same learner across successive transitions within one domain. A generative AI tutor performs three main tasks:

01. TASK GENERATION

Transition-Matched Tasks

Presents tasks matched to the SOLO transition that the learner is attempting.

02. ADAPTIVE INTERVENTION

Personalized Teaching

Generates each teaching intervention based on what the learner actually wrote, rather than following a fixed script.

03. RESPONSE CLASSIFICATION

SOLO Classification

Classifies learner responses into SOLO levels. These classifications are checked against independent human coding before they count as outcomes.

EXPERIMENTAL CONDITIONS

Three Teaching Approaches

The study compares three pedagogical approaches to investigate their effectiveness across successive SOLO transitions.

APPROACH 01

Guided Questioning

Uses targeted questions to encourage learners to examine their ideas, reflect on their understanding, and develop deeper connections.

APPROACH 02

Contrasting Cases

Presents contrasting examples or cases to help learners identify differences, recognize patterns, and understand relationships between concepts.

APPROACH 03

Worked Examples

Provides worked-out examples that demonstrate the process of solving a problem and developing an understanding of the underlying concepts.

EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN

How We Keep the Comparison Fair

A learner's understanding can change after any teaching intervention. Therefore, the approaches cannot simply be compared one after another on the same topic. Instead, the study uses three matched topics from one domain.

01. MATCHED TOPICS

Three Topics

Uses three matched topics from the same domain to avoid comparing approaches sequentially on the same topic.

02. RANDOM ASSIGNMENT

Randomized Approaches

At each transition, the three teaching approaches are randomly assigned across the three topics. Every approach gets a first attempt at the same transition.

03. FRESH ASSESSMENT

Independent Assessment

Every intervention is followed by a fresh, equivalent assessment. A topic advances only when the target transition is demonstrated.

04. REPEATED ATTEMPTS

Randomized Retry

A failed attempt receives another randomly chosen approach. Approaches are evaluated only after data collection, so no outcome influences assignment.

RESEARCH SIGNIFICANCE

Why It Matters

This study investigates whether the structure of a learner's understanding can provide a meaningful basis for selecting teaching approaches.

POSSIBLE OUTCOME 01

If Effectiveness Varies

Adaptive learning systems could gain a structural basis for choosing how to teach at different SOLO transitions. This could then be tested against other selection methods.

POSSIBLE OUTCOME 02

If Effectiveness Does Not Vary

SOLO transition state may offer limited guidance for choosing among these teaching approaches in this domain. This would also be a useful research finding.
