RESEARCH

Human-Technology
Interaction

Our research investigates how humans interact with technology and how multimodal sensing, artificial intelligence, and human-centered design can improve human cognitive well-being.

05

Context-Aware Detection of AI Overreliance Using Everyday Physiological & Behavioral Signals

Users often trust AI-generated answers even when they are incorrect. Existing methods fail to detect overreliance in real time. This project aims to identify uncritical AI trust by analyzing user behavior, such as eye movements, typing speed, mouse activity, and idle time, while accounting for factors like stress, fatigue, and lack of experience.
