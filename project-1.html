ONGOING PROJECT

Multimodal AI Assistant for Cognitive-Aware Driving

Personalized driving assistance based on the driver's cognitive state, stress, fatigue, and experience.

PROJECT

Abstract

Modern intelligent vehicles can provide assistance to drivers, but most systems do not continuously adapt to the driver's cognitive and physiological state. This project investigates how multimodal artificial intelligence can personalize driving assistance according to changes in cognitive load, stress, fatigue, and driver experience. The research explores physiological and behavioral sensing together with AI models to understand the driver's state and provide context-aware assistance.
